# Kerjaya. · Malaysian Career Platform Terminology Glossary

**Product:** Kerjaya.  
**Audience:** Malaysian students, interns, fresh graduates, early-career and experienced professionals, career changers.  
**Languages:** English (Malaysia) and Bahasa Melayu (Malaysia).  
**Authoritative Reference:** `docs/kerjaya/phase-02d/terminology-glossary.md`

---

## 1. Tone of Voice & Copywriting Principles

1. **Clear, Calm & Dignified:** Candidates are working on high-stakes documents that affect their livelihoods. Copy must be reassuring, direct, and unpretentious. Avoid hype, artificial urgency, or generic Silicon Valley buzzwords.
2. **Authentic Malaysian Context:**
   - Reference Malaysian education pathways (SPM, STPM, Matrikulasi, Diploma Politeknik, Ijazah Sarjana Muda, TVET).
   - Use Malaysian geography (Petaling Jaya, Melaka, Georgetown, Shah Alam, Cyberjaya, Kuching, Kota Kinabalu) in examples.
   - Use Malaysian Ringgit (`RM`) for all pricing.
   - Use Asia/Kuala_Lumpur time (MYT) for timestamps.
3. **Strict Bahasa Melayu (Malaysia) Standard:**
   - Adhere to Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka (DBP) standard terminology.
   - **Never use Indonesian terms** (e.g. do not use *lowongan*, *unduh*, *unggah*, *surel*, *berkas*, *pratinjau*, *karier*, *resume* spelled with odd accents, or *lamaran*).
4. **Human-in-the-Loop AI Transparency:**
   - AI suggestions are always presented as drafts for candidate review.
   - Copy must clearly say "Cadangan penulisan" (Writing suggestion) and "Semak sebelum guna" (Review before using).
   - Never claim an AI score is an employer ATS score or hiring probability.
