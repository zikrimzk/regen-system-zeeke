# Landmark reference study

## PETRONAS Twin Towers

Both towers are 88 storeys. The design uses stainless steel and glass around an eight-pointed floor plan with added curved lobes. The double-decker skybridge links levels 41 and 42 at 170 m above ground, against the 452 m tower height. The illustration therefore places its bridge in the lower-middle area measured from base to pinnacle. The review checks the pair of slender spires, vertical symmetry, stepped crown tiers, horizontal facade bands, side annexes and visible stacked bridge decks without a scene label. [Official design and structure](https://www.petronastwintowers.com.my/the-towers/design-structure/)

The park approach, foreground landscaping and optical perspective form a stylised set. It is an original illustration informed by architectural references. The website’s photographs were not imported into the generated master or public files.
