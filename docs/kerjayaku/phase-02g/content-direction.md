## 1. Editorial Philosophy & Brand Voice

Kerjayaku speaks with a supportive, clear, and dignified tone. It avoids aggressive marketing hype, manufactured urgency, fake countdown timers, and generic corporate clichés.

As a Malaysian career platform, its language reflects Malaysian reality:
- Natural Bahasa Melayu (Standard Bahasa Melayu compliant with Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka usage, eliminating Indonesian false friends such as *unduh*, *unggah*, *kata sandi*, *beranda*, *peramban*).
- Authentic Malaysian English (internationally clear, professional, respecting Malaysian career paths like internships, SPM/STPM/Diploma/Degree entry, and private/GLC/civil service recruitment contexts).
- Complete bilingual parity: every key in `InterfaceText.php` is authored symmetrically with zero mixed-language strings.
