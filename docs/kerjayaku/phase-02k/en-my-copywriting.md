- **answer2** — You can also write cover letters, organise your profile and get writing guidance. The career tools above show what else Kerjayaku is exploring.

- **answer3** — Mochi is Kerjayaku's friendly companion, designed to help you navigate the platform and use its tools.

- **answer4** — Assist Me helps you start a draft. Refine Me helps improve writing you've already prepared.

- **answer5** — Yes. You can return to your saved resume to review and update its content.
- **faq6** — Do I need to pay to use Kerjayaku?
- **answer6** — Access varies by tool. Visit the Packages page to compare the available options.
- **faq7** — Does Kerjayaku guarantee that I'll get a job?
- **answer7** — Kerjayaku supports career preparation. Hiring decisions remain with employers.
- **faq8** — How do I control optional browser storage?
- **answer8** — Use Cookie choices in the footer to review or change your optional storage settings.
