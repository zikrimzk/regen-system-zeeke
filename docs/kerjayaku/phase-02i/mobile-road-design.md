# Mobile road design

Desktop places artwork and copy on alternating sides of the route. Montage width, midpoint and vertical space follow the actual content. Phones use a short entrance bend from the hero artwork, followed by a lane with small lateral movement along the left gutter. Art and copy stack on the other side with native vertical scroll. The packages carousel is the single horizontal scrolling region on its own page.

Reduced motion removes the travelling sprite and character effects while preserving the same headings, content, landmarks and links. Static sprites are visible in the opening and closing illustrations. With JavaScript disabled, the content and page links remain in the native reading order. The second skip link jumps over the story to templates, packages and registration.
