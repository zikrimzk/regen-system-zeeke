## 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. **What happens to my personal information?**  
   *Your career information remains entirely yours. Access to saved documents requires secure sign-in. We do not sell your personal data. When you request AI writing assistance, only relevant details are processed securely.*
2. **How does pricing and payment work?**  
   *All packages are one-time payments in Malaysian Ringgit (RM) processed via HitPay (supporting FPX, debit/credit cards, and e-wallets). There are zero hidden recurring subscriptions or auto-renewals.*
3. **Do I need an account to try Kerjayaku?**  
   *No. You can explore interactive previews and test template layouts for free without an account. Creating a free account is only required when you are ready to save documents, use Mochi writing tools, or download PDFs.*
4. **Can I switch resume templates at any time?**  
   *Yes. Your career information is stored independently from visual formatting. You can switch between any of our 16 ATS templates anytime without ever having to re-enter your details.*
5. **Will AI change my resume information automatically?**  
   *No. Kerjayaku enforces ethical career integrity. Mochi, Assist Me, and Refine Me only suggest clearer, more impactful phrasing. You always retain complete control to review, edit, or reject every suggestion.*
6. **Can I use Kerjayaku on my smartphone?**  
   *Yes. Kerjayaku is built mobile-first and adapts cleanly to any smartphone or tablet screen. You can also install it to your home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on supported browsers.*
7. **What is Mochi compared to standard resume builders?**  
   *Mochi is your proactive career companion. Rather than generating generic text, Mochi guides you to identify your strongest workplace achievements, structures your experience, and conducts realistic mock interview drills.*
