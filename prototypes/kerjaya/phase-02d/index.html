Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kerjaya., templates, AI assistance, and privacy.

Can I change my template later without losing my writing?

Yes! Your profile data, work history, and custom bullets remain intact when switching between any of our 16 designs.

Does Kerjaya. work on mobile phones?

Absolutely. Kerjaya. is built as a progressive web app (PWA). You can draft on your phone in Write mode, preview the final PDF, and save seamlessly.

Will the AI rewrite my resume without my consent?

Never. You must explicitly review and accept each wording suggestion. You maintain complete editorial ownership over every word.

What happens to my account after I pay?

All package purchases are one-time payments in RM. We never automatically renew or charge your card without your explicit authorization.
